Alpha Travel – Booking Policy

Bookings are confirmed only after advance payment is received.
Customers must verify travel dates, passport validity, and required documents before final confirmation.

Cancellation Policy
	•	More than 15 days before departure: 80% refund
	•	7 to 15 days before departure: 50% refund
	•	Less than 7 days before departure: no refund

Rescheduling

Rescheduling depends on airline, hotel, and visa partner availability. Additional charges may apply.

Support Policy

Customer support is available for booking assistance, itinerary issues, and travel coordination during business hours.

Corporate Clients

Corporate travel clients receive priority support and can request customized billing and documentation.
